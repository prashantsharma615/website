The Story

Pallavi Gupta

Welcome to Sukoon by Pallavi — a boutique born from a deep love for Indian craftsmanship and contemporary style. Every piece in our collection is thoughtfully curated to bring you clothing that feels as beautiful as it looks.

From timeless Banarasi silks to modern everyday kurtas, we believe fashion should bring comfort and confidence. Our name, Sukoon, means peace — and that is exactly what we want you to feel when you wear our clothes.

— Pallavi
